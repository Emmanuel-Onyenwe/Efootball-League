League Briefing & Rules

Everything you need to know about how the Panic Keh eFootball League operates.

Match Days & Deadlines

League matches run Monday to Friday. Opponents may schedule matches at any mutually agreed time during the day.

The strict final deadline for all daily matches is 1:00 AM.

Tagging & Walkovers

Tag your opponent early. Between 9:00 PM and 11:00 PM, a 3-hour response margin is enforced. Do not wait until your opponent is asleep.

If properly tagged within the required time and they fail to respond, keep your message proof. Admins may award a 3-0 walkover upon review. Walkovers are never automatic without evidence.

Settings & Disconnects

Matches are 10 minutes, Excellent condition, using official settings. Smart Assist is STRICTLY PROHIBITED. Cheating or exploiting glitches will not be tolerated.

If a disconnect occurs, do not restart 0-0. Reconnect and play only the remaining time (e.g., if disconnected at 35 minutes, play a 6-minute match). Goals scored prior to the disconnect carry over.

NO SMART ASSIST 🚫

Submissions & Penalties

The winner must upload the result to the website AND post the final score plus player ratings in WhatsApp (tagging opponent and admins). In a draw, the Home Team uploads. Squads used in-game must match your profile squad screenshot.

Participation is compulsory. Ghosting or missing fixtures results in strikes. 2 strikes equals elimination. The prize pool will be distributed among the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners at the end of the season.

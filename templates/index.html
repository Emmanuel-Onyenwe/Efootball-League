League Briefing & Rules

Everything you need to know about how the Panic Keh eFootball League operates.

Table Glossary

  • P (Played): Total matches completed.
  • W, D, L: Matches Won (3 pts), Drawn (1 pt), and Lost (0 pts).
  • GD (Goal Difference): Total goals scored minus total goals conceded. Used as the first tiebreaker.
  • Pts (Points): Total league points earned.
  • PPG (Points Per Game): Your Total Points divided by your Matches Played. This is the ultimate equalizer! If you play fewer games because an opponent was eliminated, PPG ensures your rank is perfectly balanced and fair.

Match Submissions

All match results must be submitted via the Submit Result tab. You must attach a clear screenshot of the final post-match stats screen as proof. The Admin will review the screenshot before the table is officially updated.

Failing to play scheduled fixtures before the deadline will result in an administrative strike.

Eliminations & The "Bye Week"

If a player ghosts the tournament, becomes inactive, or hits 2 Strikes, they are eliminated.

What is a "Bye Week"? If you were scheduled to play an eliminated player, that match is completely erased from the schedule. You get a "bye" (a rest week).

You do not get a free 1-0 win or free points. Instead, the match just doesn't happen. Thanks to the PPG system, you are never punished for playing fewer games. The math automatically balances the table so everything remains 100% fair. Any past matches the eliminated player actually completed will remain on the board.

League Briefing & Rules

Everything you need to know about how the Panic Keh eFootball League operates. Tap any section to read the details.

Table Glossary
  • P (Played): Total matches completed.
  • W, D, L: Matches Won (3 pts), Drawn (1 pt), and Lost (0 pts).
  • GF (Goals For): Total number of goals scored by a player.
  • GD (Goal Difference): Total goals scored minus total goals conceded. Used as the first tiebreaker.
  • Pts (Points): Total league points earned.
  • PPG (Points Per Game): Your Total Points divided by your Matches Played. This is the ultimate equalizer! If you play fewer games because an opponent was eliminated, PPG ensures your rank is perfectly balanced and fair.
Match Submissions & Deadlines

Step 1: The WINNER must post the final score screenshot in the WhatsApp group and tag the opponent to confirm (If a draw, the HOME player posts).
Step 2: Once confirmed, that same player logs in and submits the result via the Submit Result tab.

Active players are free to play ahead. You have strictly 24 hours to clear your pending fixture.

Network Disconnects

If the network drops, state the score and half in WhatsApp immediately. We do NOT restart 0-0.

• Before 75th Min: Start a new match, play ONE HALF (45 mins), quit, and add the new goals to the original score.
• 75th Min or Later: If leading by 2+ goals, the match is over (Leader takes 3 pts). If leading by 1 goal or tied, play ONE HALF (45 mins) to settle it.

Penalties & Eliminations

Ghosting: Dodging matches results in 1 Strike and a 3-0 Walkover loss. If both players fail to make an effort within 24 hours, the match is VOIDED (0 points, 0 goals).

Elimination: Hitting 2 Strikes gets you eliminated. If you are scheduled to play an eliminated player, that match is erased from the schedule (a "Bye Week"). You are not punished for playing fewer games; the PPG system automatically balances the table fairly.
